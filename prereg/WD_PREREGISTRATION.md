# Stage 5 Pre-Registration: Pushing on the Weight-Decay Law

Written and committed **before any Stage 5 run existed**. The predictions in
[`wd_predictions.json`](wd_predictions.json) were generated by
`python -m grokking.src.wd_analysis --freeze-predictions`, which refuses to overwrite them, and the
analysis script reads them from that file. The git history is the timestamp: this file and the
predictions arrive in the same commit as the experiment code, before any file under `runs/wd_push/`.
Anything changed after that commit is listed under *Deviations* below.

## Why

Stage 4B found grokking time ∝ `wd^β` with β = −0.964, 95% CI [−1.049, −0.879] (seed fixed effects,
`src/robustness.py`). That matches a known result: Lyu et al. (2024) prove $t_g = \frac{1}{\lambda} \log \alpha$
for homogeneous networks with large initialisation scale α and small weight decay λ. Matching the
1/λ shape alone does not distinguish this theory from other explanations. These experiments test the
parts that do, and they test whether the weight norm, a signal that needs no reverse engineering,
forecasts grokking for individual seeds.

## Fixed settings

- Task, model and optimiser as in Stages 2–4: (a + b) mod 113, 30% training split, one-layer
  transformer, full-batch AdamW, β = (0.9, 0.98). One epoch is one optimiser step.
- Seeds 0–4 throughout. Metrics logged every 100 epochs.
- **Grokking time**: first logged epoch with test accuracy ≥ 0.95. A run that never reaches it is
  *censored* at its budget: reported as not grokked, never imputed.
- Runs: `python -m grokking.src.wd_experiments`. Analysis: `python -m grokking.src.wd_analysis`.

## Hypotheses and decision rules

**H0 — sanity (gate).** At lr 1e-3, wd 1.0, α = 1, init = split = seed, each seed's grokking time
lies in (old − 500, old], where *old* is its Stage 4B value logged every 500 epochs.
If any seed fails, stop and find out why before interpreting H1–H5.

**H1 — the clock is lr × wd (E2).** AdamW shrinks every weight by a factor (1 − lr·wd) per step, so if
the weight-decay phase is the bottleneck, grokking time should depend on the product lr·wd only.
Fit log t = a_seed + b_lr·log lr + b_wd·log wd.
*Supported* if both 95% CIs include −1. *Rejected* for lr if the b_lr CI excludes −1.
Also reported: grokking times in the three cells with lr·wd = 1e-3, and the median per-seed
forecast error |log(observed / predicted)| against the table below.

**H2 — grokking time rises with log α (E1).** Fit t = a_seed + slope·ln α.
*Direction supported* if the slope's 95% CI lies above 0.
*Quantitatively consistent* with Lyu et al.'s leading order if the CI includes
1000 epochs per unit ln α (= 1 / (lr·wd)).
Caveat fixed now: α = 0.5 lies outside the large-initialisation regime the theory covers.

**H3 — 1/wd extrapolates (E3).** Per-seed predictions below.
*Supported* if at least 80% of the 30 runs are correct: grokked within a factor of 1.5 of the
prediction, or did not grok where the prediction exceeds the 120k budget (including all wd = 0 runs).
Where the law breaks at large wd, that is reported as its edge, not explained away.

**H4 — initialisation vs split (E4). Exploratory, no directional prediction.** Two-way decomposition
of log grokking time over the 5 × 5 grid (F-tests with the interaction as error, df 4 and 16), and
key-frequency Jaccard overlap for pairs sharing only the init, only the split, or neither.

**H5 — weight-norm alarm (all runs).** Alarm: first logged epoch where weight_l2_norm <= 0.90 * running max.
Expected from Stage 4B: it fires before grokking in nearly every grokked run but mainly reflects the
configuration, not the seed. Fit log t = a_config + slope·log t_alarm.
*Supported* if the slope's 95% CI excludes 1. Also reported: false alarms (alarm fired, no grokking
within budget) and misses (grokked with no alarm).

## Known limitations, stated in advance

- Five seeds per cell; one task, one architecture, one training fraction.
- α ∈ {0.5, 1, 2, 4} is a coarse grid; large α may destabilise early training.
- lr = 2e-3 may destabilise training; that would be reported, not dropped.
- Grokking time resolution is 100 epochs.
